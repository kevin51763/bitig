Hayagrīva’s location in the maṇḍala and position with regard to other krodharāja-s vary according to the context and the Tantric system. Usually he has to be visualized in his wrathful appearance, sometimes in connection with Bhṛkuṭī (especially in the sādhana-s of Khasarpaṇa and Padmanarteśvara, two forms of Avalokiteśvara), other times in connection with Yamāntaka, Aparājita and Amṛtakuṇḍali (see occurrences below).

Afterwards it is taught how these 32 nimitta-s relate to the 4 kinds of nimitta discussed before. Here, the latter are specified as being the four basic kinds of nimitta (mūlanimitta).\nThe first two kinds, viz. nimitta in the sense of the “meditation object”  (ālambananimitta) and nimitta in the sense of “cause” (nidānanimitta), represent two basic types that are also used elsewhere in the Samāhitā Bhūmiḥ to explain the term nimitta in the context of meditation theory.
